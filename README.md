## 📊 Featured Projects

### [Retail Sales & Customer Analytics (Favourita)]https://github.com/tolushona1/Favorita-Retail-Sales-Analytics

SQL + Power BI project analysing a multi-year Ecuadorian grocery retail dataset, covering overall sales performance, product performance, store/regional performance and the impact of promotions.

### [Northwind Traders Executive Dashboard] https://github.com/tolushona1/Northwind-Traders-Executive-Dashboard

Interactive Tableau dashboard covering revenue, customers, orders, shipping and discounts. Surfaced a customer concentration risk - the top three customers accounted for £113k-£118k each, followed by a sharp drop-off.

### [UK House Prices vs Regional Employment] https://github.com/tolushona1/UK-House-Prices-Analysis

Merged UK Land Registry house price data with ONS employment data across 9 English regions using Excel/XLOOKUP, uncovering a 0.79 correlation between regional house prices and employment rates.
